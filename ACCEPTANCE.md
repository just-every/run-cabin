# First release

- An owner signs in and manages properties/team access; managers operate the portfolio and cleaners see only assigned jobs.
- A manager imports Airbnb and Booking.com calendars, reviews a flagged clash and feed freshness, then approves exact snapshots without silently adopting external edits.
- A manager approves a cleaner's turnover; that cleaner completes every checklist item with a private uploaded photograph.
- A manager prepares and approves an owner's monthly AUD statement and downloads a frozen, formula-safe CSV.
- An agent prepares calendar, turnover and statement drafts; a person approves, with actor and exact revision recorded.
- An owner previews and approves the Cabin property-register move-in kit, reads its durable journal and safely undoes unchanged imports.
- An owner exports/restores records and photos, erases personal details and uses a later erasure journal to prevent an old backup restoring them.

Not needed: channel APIs, double-booking prevention, guest payments, trust accounting, pricing pushes, guest messaging, provider calls, custom branding, docs site or a registered domain.

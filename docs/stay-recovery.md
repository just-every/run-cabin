Open **Stay recovery** when a cleaner cancels, the home is not ready, checkout runs late or a guest reports a problem. Cleaners can report against their own turnovers; a guest can report from their private stay page. The case stays with the affected arrival and the turnover that prepares the home for it. An open case also leads the **Stay recovery** panel beside the **Calendar**, where you can draft its plan, approve or reject its proposal, and see what it does to the owner's balance.

## Set the owner's limits

Under **Recovery settings by home**, the owner selects allowed backup cleaners and sets separate guest credit and extra cleaning limits in USD. Enable automatic recovery to apply a proposed action within both limits without another approval. Limits include prior recovery credits and cleaning charges for the same stay. Only the owner can change these settings or approve an amount above them.

## Put the stay right

1. Open the case and select **Find replacement and draft plan**. Cabin chooses an active cleaner from the home's list with no overlapping assigned turnover. If none is available, it asks the owner to arrange another window. You can also prepare a plan yourself.
2. Review the replacement cleaner, credit, extra cleaning charge, explanation and exact guest message. Approval binds the current stay, recipient, home settings, cleaner and owner limits. If any changes, prepare the proposal again. Rejecting a proposal applies nothing.
3. Approval reassigns the turnover and emails the replacement cleaner their window, address and cleaner code. It replaces the guest code, keeps their private stay link working and sends corrected scheduled messages only when their contents changed. The credit offer goes to the guest. Credit and extra cleaning charges become a frozen owner adjustment statement; Cabin records the credit and does not process a refund or transfer funds.
4. The replacement cleaner signs in, accepts the case, and completes the turnover checklist with the required photos under **Turnovers**.
5. Confirm the new code on the physical keypad if no smart-lock account is connected. Then select **Check readiness and close**. Blocking maintenance, missing photos, unaccepted cleaning, unconfirmed access or an undelivered credit offer prevent closure. The case closes after the guest readiness email is accepted. Check **Inbox** and **Statements** for delivery failures.

The owner can undo an applied or closed recovery. Cabin restores an unfinished assignment, issues a fresh access code, records an exact reversing owner adjustment and sends a correction to the guest. Completed work and its photos remain in the history; accepted emails cannot be recalled. All actions retain their reason and approving revision.

## Optional AI wording

**Stay recovery → Set up or manage AI wording** works before adding homes or stays. Save an Anthropic API key from your own account; Cabin encrypts it and shares the connection with Inbox. Test it from the same page. On an unapplied proposal, **Draft AI opening** adds a courteous opening for your review. AI cannot choose recipients, cleaners, money, access or readiness. Provider failures are shown and do not change the recovery action. No key is needed for deterministic recovery.

On RER Managed, AI wording and smart-lock provider calls are not available yet. Self-hosted installs use their own provider accounts. Without a lock account, set the generated code on the keypad and confirm it in the case. Relocation, automatic rescheduling, provider-confirmed live lock proof, emergency handling and a complete trust-accounting subledger remain outside this recovery flow.

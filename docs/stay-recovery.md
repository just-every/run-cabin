Open **Stay recovery** when a cleaner cancels, the home is not ready, checkout runs late or a guest reports a problem. Cleaners can report against their own turnovers; a guest can report from their private stay page. The case stays with the affected arrival and the turnover that prepares the home for it. An open case also leads the **Stay recovery** panel beside the **Calendar**, where you can draft its plan, approve or reject its proposal, and see what it does to the owner's balance.

## Set the owner's limits

Under **Recovery settings by home**, the owner selects allowed backup cleaners and sets separate guest credit and extra cleaning limits in USD. Enable automatic recovery to apply a proposed action within both limits without another approval. Limits include prior recovery credits and cleaning charges for the same stay. Only the owner can change these settings or approve an amount above them.

## Put the stay right

1. Open the case and select **Find replacement and draft plan**. Cabin chooses an active cleaner from the home's list with no overlapping assigned turnover. If none is available, it asks the owner to arrange another window. You can also prepare a plan yourself.
2. Review the replacement cleaner, credit, extra cleaning charge, explanation and exact guest message. Approval binds the current stay, recipient, home settings, cleaner and owner limits. If any changes, prepare the proposal again. Rejecting a proposal applies nothing.
3. Approval reassigns the turnover and emails the replacement cleaner their window, address and cleaner code. It replaces the guest code, keeps their private stay link working and sends corrected scheduled messages only when their contents changed. The credit offer goes to the guest. Credit and extra cleaning charges become a frozen owner adjustment statement; Cabin records the credit and does not process a refund or transfer funds.
4. The replacement cleaner signs in, accepts the case, and completes the turnover checklist with the required photos under **Turnovers**.
5. Confirm the new code on the physical keypad if no smart-lock account is connected. Then select **Check readiness and close**. Blocking maintenance, missing photos, unaccepted cleaning, unconfirmed access or an undelivered credit offer prevent closure. The readiness email starts the guest check. The verified guest confirms that the home and access are working on their private stay page, then a person records a source-linked follow-up check. Only then can the case close. Check **Inbox** and **Statements** for delivery failures.

The owner can undo an applied or closed recovery that kept the same home and dates. An approved alternative instead needs a fresh exact reservation and balance correction; undo cannot silently restore a different owner or inventory. Cabin restores an unfinished assignment, issues a fresh access code, records an exact reversing owner adjustment and sends a correction to the guest. Completed work and its photos remain in the history; accepted emails cannot be recalled. All actions retain their reason and approving revision.

## Optional AI wording

**Stay recovery → Set up or manage AI wording** works before adding homes or stays. Save an Anthropic API key from your own account; Cabin encrypts it and shares the connection with Inbox. Test it from the same page. On an unapplied proposal, **Draft AI opening** adds a courteous opening for your review. AI cannot choose recipients, cleaners, money, access or readiness. Provider failures are shown and do not change the recovery action. No key is needed for deterministic recovery.

On RER Managed, AI wording and smart-lock provider calls are not available yet. Self-hosted installs use their own provider accounts. Without a lock account, set the generated code on the keypad and confirm it in the case. Seam code creation, changed windows and removal retain durable claims and reconcile provider readback after interruption. An API acknowledgement alone never marks a code set; current readback must match the device, PIN and exact window, with no pending mutation or provider error. Seam may report a future code as unset while confirming its native schedule on the device; that is confirmed programming, and the code remains inactive until its start. See [the provider access-code object](https://www.seam.co/docs/api/access_codes/object). If provider removal stays in progress, inspect the exact code in Seam and request account sync there if needed. Cabin still waits for confirmed removal; a sync acknowledgement cannot close the case. Physical entry still needs a person. Emergency handling uses the separate guest-issue flow. Refunds and owner distributions retain their existing financial approval and provider prerequisites.

## A revised arrival and future-stay rate

The owner can enable one-use 10% future-stay rent offers in each home’s recovery policy. Publish the home’s direct-booking page, permit returning offers under Guests, verify the guest’s contact with its source and record their explicit consent first. A recovery proposal can withdraw an early arrival to normal check-in and include an expiring future-stay rate. Review the exact wording before approval; changed stay facts, consent, guest identity or booking terms require a fresh review. No usable offer exists before approval.

Approval changes the stay’s arrival, guest code and preceding cleaner window; the scheduled check-in message follows those facts. It emails the exact revised arrangements. On the private stay page the guest accepts or asks to speak with the host. A help request remains escalated and notifies the host. Acceptance permits closing only after the other readiness checks pass. It does not mark the home ready.

The guest’s accepted page links to the private discounted booking page. Availability, stay rules, contact, consent and terms are checked again at checkout. The 10% applies only to rent before tax; cleaning and extras remain separate. The charged return and owner statement use the reduced rent. Undo revokes an unused offer; an already redeemed offer requires review of the paid return rather than silently changing that booking. General promotions, paid extras and gap-night extensions keep their own acceptance in CONTRACT.md.

## Conflicts and alternative stays

A confirmed stay that overlaps another held stay or block opens one durable recovery case. Its evidence links the current reservation history, guest thread, field job, photos, access outcomes and frozen owner adjustment. Declined requests never become conflicting bookings.

Expand **Offer another home or different dates** in the proposal. Cabin checks the exact destination nights, both homes’ current recovery cleaner lists, the cleaner’s property grants and capacity, source revisions, terms and preparation window again at approval. Changed evidence requires a new proposal. Alternatives always need explicit manager approval; automatic recovery grants do not approve relocation or a different guest promise. The guest accepts the exact alternative on their private stay page.

An uncharged native reservation can move through the existing reservation amendment door. Where no preceding turnover exists, Cabin creates an arrival preparation with a real vacant window, cleaner acceptance, checklist, photos and named readiness inspection. Moving dates invalidates earlier completion. Historical owner postings remain frozen; approved recovery spend is charged to the original owner through a balanced adjustment and never invents a cash transfer.

A reservation whose channel or paid checkout controls its terms remains explicitly escalated until the operator reconciles and obtains source acceptance. Cabin does not substitute a local move for a changed channel reservation or signed paid booking. Connected channel closures require accepted receipts; obsolete closures need approved reopening. Uncertain outcomes, incomplete removal of the previous code, unresolved guest work, missing readiness evidence or an unconfirmed owner adjustment keep the recovery open.

## Interrupted messages

Guest replies and team notices retain the due time, attempt, claim and provider result. An expired claim becomes an assigned uncertain failure and is never automatically resent. In the Inbox, the installation owner can reconcile an uncertain reply against provider records. Confirmed acceptance records the provider reference; proof that no effect occurred permits a separate exact retry. A late response cannot overwrite a newer decision. This local reference is not a promise that the mail provider deduplicates submissions.

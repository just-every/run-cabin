Identity verification is configured under **Direct bookings → Home booking settings → Guest identity policy**. The same saved property policy covers direct and channel reservations. Choose every reservation, none, or any of three factual risk criteria: short booking lead time, party size, and reservation value. A channel booking missing a fact needed by an enabled criterion requires review. Cabin does not invent a risk score.

Assign an active manager or the installation owner. Independently choose whether verification blocks direct-booking confirmation and whether it withholds guest door access. Each reservation keeps its evaluated policy, reasons, reviewer and retention deadline; changing the home policy does not rewrite that decision. Existing legacy direct settings continue to require Stripe verification before confirmation and door access, with the owner responsible for review.

Guests open **Complete identity check or request manual review** on their private booking or arrival page. Stripe Identity requires explicit consent before Cabin opens its hosted document check. Stripe receives the documents; Cabin stores only the verification session reference, mode and outcome. Cabin never retrieves document images, extracted fields or verification reports. A return URL is not proof of success: Cabin reads the session's actual status. Stripe test results do not establish live Identity eligibility or a real guest's identity.

The guest can instead request and consent to human review. The assigned reviewer arranges the check directly, then records an approval or rejection with the method, a minimal evidence reference and reason. Do not put document numbers, images or sensitive personal details in these fields or the guest inbox. A manual approval is labelled as a human decision; it does not change Stripe's outcome to verified. Only the assigned current manager or installation owner may inspect the review details or decide. The installation owner may reassign a case. Other staff receive only operational status; cleaners and agents receive no verification case data.

Incomplete and unsuccessful checks remain in **Assigned identity reviews**, with the responsible reviewer notified. Required unresolved checks, including rejected/manual cases, obey the saved access policy. Door-code creation, guest pages, lock dispatch and outgoing access messages must all use that same gate. Human approval releases only this identity requirement; rental agreements, readiness and other access requirements still apply.

Cabin removes the guest capability, consent, manual evidence and identity history at guest erasure or the saved number of days after checkout. A private redaction task retains the Stripe reference only until Stripe confirms removal of collected verification data. Redaction can be asynchronous; processing and uncertain outcomes stay visible rather than pretending deletion succeeded. Cabin requests Stripe's redaction endpoint and polls its reported redaction status. References are then removed from both the case and direct-stay record. Existing signed rental agreements and financial legal records follow their separate retention policies.

Self-hosted installations reuse their existing Stripe test/live connection. No new verification partner account is required. RER Managed installations still cannot call Stripe directly; the manual alternative remains a human decision and does not simulate a partner check.

Provider implementation follows Stripe's official [VerificationSession API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/identity/verification_sessions) and [redaction API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/identity/verification_sessions/redact). Test provider responses belong only at the network edge; real hosted success/failure acceptance must use the owner's Stripe Identity test account.

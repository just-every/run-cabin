# Native saved pricing

In Direct bookings, open a home's **Pricing settings**. Save its nightly floor,
weekly percentage and monthly percentage. Then save each channel's percentage or
fixed USD nightly adjustment. Percentage adjustments can include cleaning and
extra-guest fees independently. Fixed adjustments exclude fees; selecting fees
with a fixed adjustment is refused before anything is saved. Changes require the
current home revision and an active human manager or owner.

The source is the home's explicitly saved native nightly and dated rates.
These settings do not connect a PRICE-001 provider. The channel preview is a local
calculation, with no OTA publication, acknowledgment or settlement claim.

Each night uses the saved native rate, then the channel adjustment, then the
length discount. Seven through 27 nights use weekly savings; 28 nights and above
use monthly savings instead. Length discounts affect rent only. Amounts round to
the nearest cent at each nightly step, with a half cent rounded up. The nightly
percentage discount amount is rounded before subtraction. Percentage
fee adjustments round each selected stay fee independently. Every final night
is at least the listing's saved floor. The breakdown names every floor conflict
and retains the requested amount as well as the enforced amount.

Existing private returning offers follow the length discount, with the floor
still enforced. Coupons follow the length calculation and are refused if any
night would cross its floor. Guests cannot choose a channel, supply a price or
override the server's saved calculation in a direct quote or checkout.

The staff channel preview shows rent, fees, lodging taxes and a guest total.
Its expected payout subtracts the explicitly entered channel commission from
rent and fees only; lodging taxes and the separate damage authorization are
excluded. Processor fees, provider-specific commission bases and actual payout
reconciliation remain outside this estimate. Direct has zero channel commission.

Public quotes, staff quote versions and signed direct stays retain the exact
calculation: native rate and dated-rate ID per night, selected channel adjustment,
included fee choices, weekly and monthly percentages, chosen discount, floor,
requested and final amounts, home revision and pricing-policy revision.
A booking-settings edit retains these settings. A pricing edit invalidates
unsigned checkout and staff quote acceptance; it never reprices a signed stay.
Restart retains both signed calculations and earlier staff quote versions.

Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com rate publication, accepted readback, discount plans,
unsupported provider combinations, externally booked reservation provenance and
actual commission and payout reconciliation require the authorized provider
accounts. PRICE-002 remains partial until those original outcomes are proven.

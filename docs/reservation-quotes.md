# Reservation quotes

In Reservations, open Guest quotes, choose a published direct-booking home, enter the guest name/email, dates and guest count, then set an expiry with an explicit time zone (for example `2026-11-01T18:00:00Z`). Choose whether to hold the nights. Issue and email quote saves the itemized USD price and cancellation terms and queues the private review link through the existing guest-mail sender. The card shows its actual email outcome; development suppresses delivery.

Use Revise dates, guests or expiry to price a new version against current home terms. Prior versions stay visible. A revised hold replaces the previous one. Expire quote releases its nights immediately; time-based expiry runs on the five-minute Cron and before booking requests/commits. Expiry is inclusive at the stored UTC instant. An unheld quote promises no inventory.

The guest opens the private link, reviews rent, cleaning, extra-guest fees, each tax, the separate damage authorization and cancellation policy, then signs and continues to the existing Stripe checkout. Changed price, agreement, management terms, occupancy or availability require review before payment. Quote consumption and replacement of its hold happen in one guarded transaction. A repeated or concurrent acceptance cannot create another reservation or checkout. If Stripe creation is interrupted, the saved booking page supports the existing payment reconciliation/retry flow.

Keep quote links private. Staff links use a purpose-specific human door; ordinary API/agent outputs omit the capability. Cleaner accounts cannot issue or read quotes. Guest/property erasure disables affected links; portable archives retain terms but expire quote capabilities and cancel quote inventory holds. Restoring history never replays an old offer or payment.

Proof: `tests/reservations.test.mjs` covers issuance, revisions, stale/changed terms, holds, timed/manual expiry, restart, concurrency, signed payment reconciliation and competing availability. `tests/quotes-browser.test.mjs` drives Reservations, guest review and one signed checkout at 1440 and 390; only the Stripe network/UI edge is synthetic. `tests/quotes-archive.test.mjs` exercises the public export/import CLI with retained quote terms, revoked links, released holds and no replayed provider action. This proof makes no claim of a live processor charge or delivered guest email.

# Verified arrival readiness

Open **Readiness** and choose the arrival day. Completed cleaning and its required photos are necessary but do not certify building safety. Configured dispatch work must also have been accepted by the cleaner.

Each property's **Supply requirements and inspector** sets its restock items and names the installation owner or an active manager as inspector. The default items are linens, toiletries, coffee and paper goods. Changing requirements requires fresh arrival observations.

Under **Review arrival evidence**, a manager records each physical supply and entry check as passed or failed with evidence. A failed or short item keeps the arrival unready. Close blocking maintenance with its work evidence. Only the named inspector can accept the final inspection after the other checks pass. This is human verification: it does not pretend a manually configured keypad was verified through a lock provider.

A manager can explicitly except an item, recording both the reason and the guest impact/agreed mitigation. Exceptions remain visible with the person's name and time. Restocking an excepted item and passing it requires renewed inspection. Cleaning completion and required photos cannot be bypassed by a readiness exception.

Cards show at-risk arrivals and overdue arrivals whose check-in has passed. **Chase overdue or pending work** records the responsible person and actual follow-up action; it is a durable work log, not an automatically delivered notice. Observations, exceptions and follow-up remain in the property's own records and exports. Earlier observations remain readable after changes. Stay dates, cleaning, blockers, requirements or access evidence changing invalidate current observations; a concurrent or stale form is refused.

Early-arrival approval and recovery readiness confirmation use the same readiness decision. A photo upload alone cannot make these promises.

# Management agreements and allocations

Under **Properties & people → Management agreements**, save a new effective-dated agreement and enable its structured allocation model. Choose the commission base (rent, rent less channel fees, or receipts excluding collected tax), percentage and fixed per-stay fee, reserve percentage, owner-use cleaning amount and responsibility for pass-through and property-tax expenses. Enter each owner's interest in basis points, totaling 10,000, and the account mappings reviewed by the named accountant. The operator records the accountant's review; Cabin does not provide an accounting opinion.

Each new reservation freezes the complete agreement selected by arrival date. Amendments affect later reservations and never rewrite earlier stays, approved invoices or allocations. The agreement's designated contact receives the combined property statement; its owner-interest breakdown remains visible to authorized operators.

In **Statements → Agreement allocations**, select a stay with matched, settled receipts. Enter the itemized rent, pass-through receipts and collected tax from the original receipt; these must equal imported gross receipts. A draft explains every owner's receipts, channel fee, management fee, collected tax, reserve and payable. Exact approval rejects changed reservations or receipts, posts balanced reclassifications in **Trust ledger**, and cannot repeat. Collected tax already assessed to a receivable settles that receivable instead of creating a second tax liability. Reserves remain owner liabilities, not management income or a bank transfer.

In **Shared invoice approval**, attach the vendor/invoice reference and original invoice text, select its accounting date and category, and allocate the invoice across properties in basis points. Every property's dated agreement and accountant-reviewed mapping must still match at approval. Each property's cost follows its frozen owner interests or the agreement's manager-borne pass-through/tax rule. Largest-remainder allocation assigns every cent exactly once. An approved vendor/invoice reference cannot be approved twice. Costs attach to their property period; accepted work-order invoices also retain their task/reservation, original invoice photos and agreement-based owner split. Completed owner-use cleaning uses the saved cleaning rate and owner interests.

Monthly statements for structured agreements consume approved allocations, including shared costs for the month, rather than recomputing the old gross-only commission. Their CSV retains the agreement references, tax, reserve and owner-interest totals. Approval freezes the source set. The ledger and allocation evidence remain the authoritative accounting record; no allocation sends a payment, executes a distribution or files a tax return. Provider settlement connectivity and those external workflows retain their separate contract statuses.

`tests/allocations.test.mjs` exercises source-backed revenue, amendments, two-owner splits, fixed fees, reserves, shared invoices, stale approvals, balanced posting, frozen statement export and restricted reads. Existing work-order and owner-block route journeys cover invoice evidence and completion-based cleaning.

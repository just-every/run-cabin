# Team access

The installation owner manages team invitations in Properties & people. Rental ownership never grants installation administration. Choose the PMS role, homes and individual allowed actions before saving a new team member. Unsupported actions are refused. Team settings and provider/agent credentials remain installation-owner-only.

Portfolio managers, reservation staff, guest-service staff, accountants, inspectors, cleaners, maintenance vendors and rental owners have distinct action selections. The authoritative choices are in `src/cabin/permissions/policy.ts`; a role label alone never grants an action. Service roles retain assignment-based work/photo access and receive their necessary instructions, without guest identity evidence or owner financial data. Restricted operational staff see only the names and assignment identifiers of eligible colleagues, not colleagues’ grants or other homes.

Existing manager/cleaner grants, and the compatible owner-only role-form command, retain their existing role authority as an explicitly stored whole-portfolio grant. Existing manager checks still exclude installation administration, and cleaners still see only their own published assignments. The team form creates explicit home/action lists; editing a legacy grant can replace its broad grant with those lists.

Property scope is enforced on both human command transports and the actual records changed inside the transaction. Supplying a permitted home beside another home's record ID does not authorize it. Native state, pages, reports, access capabilities, statement/ agreement/photo/quote downloads and report artifacts enforce the same current grant. Private doors without a scoped implementation are refused. The grant revision is fenced before and after commits, including in-flight writes.

Rental-owner invitations require an existing current interest: the property's primary owner or a co-owner in its effective-dated management agreement. Co-owner reads require an explicit active rental-owner grant for that home. Primary-owner personal-stay/expense actions still require the existing recorded owner agreement and their individual action grants. Native owner sessions use `/app` and `/app/rental-owner/` for their scoped portal and downloads. Standalone owner sessions retain FIN-005’s current-interest and exact historical-statement grants. Ownership is rechecked on each request; a scheduled transfer does not preserve old native rights.

Offboarding refuses the next app/API request, invalidates captured delegated grants and revokes the member's issued code records. Connected locks receive removal through the existing Seam boundary; a provider acknowledgment is recorded separately from physically testing the door. Manual keypad removal remains a host action, as OPS-005 already states. Pending guest replies, quotes, statements, channel closures and scheduled reports retain their initiating authority and recheck it before an effect. Deterministic system automation retains its separately granted recovery/provider authority.

API-001 and MOBILE-001 must apply these same property/action grants and offboarding to their future app credentials and device sessions. Broader live lock verification remains OPS-005/007/008’s own requirement.

# Monthly owner close

Configure each home's legal entity in Trust ledger before importing settlements. In Statements, import Airbnb and Vrbo transaction reports or [reviewed Booking.com and Stripe USD settlement rows](statement-settlements.md). Every transaction retains its provider identity and batch; a changed or duplicate source never creates a second receipt. These imports do not claim a live channel connection or bank acknowledgement.

Use **Reconciled monthly ledger close** for an owner/month. Resolve unmatched/unassigned receipts and missing settlement evidence for departed stays first. Post actual invoices, commission, reserve allocations, tax responsibility, refunds and distributions through the existing reviewed ledger/allocation doors. A statement reports those immutable postings; it never estimates a missing fee, sends money or invents a bank receipt.

The effective-date close freezes opening owner payable, each trust journal, received settlement lines split by the reservation's saved owner interests, gross/fees/commission, tax, expense, reserve, refund, recorded distributions and closing payable. Settlement coverage and payable activity are separate, so a receipt is not added twice. The closing balance equals opening plus all posted owner activity. An exact approval checks journals, sources and agreements again, publishes to the scoped owner portal and queues one email. Both the manager and owner can download frozen CSV and paginated PDF.

An installation owner can save a monthly local-time schedule on day 1–28 of the following month. **Review** creates a draft for a human; **automatic release** uses the saved owner's current authority to approve only a successfully reconciled exact revision. A source or access failure stays visible with the responsible person's address. Five-minute Cron resumes after restart; successful generation advances the saved month, preventing repeated documents or emails. Pause stops future generation. Restored schedules are disabled until the new installation owner explicitly saves authorization.

Correct original journals with linked reversals in an open accounting period. A later month's close links back to statements containing the corrected journal. For additional same-month evidence, enter the latest approved statement ID to create a supplement containing only new activity and the updated closing payable. Original statements, recipient, terms, CSV and PDF remain unchanged. Reserve and distribution figures are records, not payment execution; ACH rails and bank reconciliation remain FIN-006.

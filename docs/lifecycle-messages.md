# Lifecycle messages

Guest inbox → Lifecycle messages by home configures booking confirmation, payment, current rental signature and identity reminders, check-in instructions, arrival checks, checkout, review requests and consented repeat-stay follow-up. Defaults remain available for homes without an override. Each template uses the reservation and the property's local time; event rules start at reservation creation. Hosts choose allowed connected channels, a responsible manager and optional local quiet hours.

Payments and required tasks are checked again at submission. Access codes and stay capabilities are withheld until payment, current agreements, identity and the arrival's cleaning/readiness evidence pass. A newly released code produces corrected instructions once. Returning invitations require the guest's explicit offers consent and the home's permission. Direct checkout uses its existing definitive payment confirmation instead of duplicating it.

Changing or cancelling a stay, turning off a rule, changing its channel policy or recording a guest opt-out re-evaluates waiting work. Emails include a private preference page that can only stop automatic lifecycle messages; it exposes no stay details or access. Staff can record the guest's request in their thread. An opt-out follows the contact at that home until explicitly changed; essential arrangements then require direct contact with the host.

Due instants, claims, submission attempts, content and outcomes persist in D1. The five-minute Cron and product changes submit eligible work; property-local quiet hours defer its eligible instant. The Inbox shows submission delay, including after a restart. An expired window is visibly skipped with its delay. Missing or disabled delivery routes and failed or interrupted submissions create a manager-assigned notice. An interrupted started attempt remains uncertain and is never retried automatically. A person must check provider non-delivery and record its evidence before sending it again.

Email is usable with the installation's own mail provider. Text and OTA messaging still require their named external account prerequisites; enabling a template does not establish those connections. Tests exercise native HTTP commands and Cron with only the mail/network transport replaced at its boundary (`tests/lifecycle.test.mjs`, `tests/inbox.test.mjs`).

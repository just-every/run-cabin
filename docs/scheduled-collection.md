# Reservation payments

Open Direct bookings → Payments · scheduled collection for a priced direct reservation created by staff. Its cancellation policy and guest email must already be saved. Existing direct website checkouts have their own payment flow and cannot be charged again here. Channel reservations require their dedicated payment mandate. Guest-card consent also applies to PAY-006 property-collect cards when that intake ships. Booking.com VCCs do not use guest consent: PAY-005’s fixed activation date, expiry and loaded-amount limits govern them. Both channel intake/collection flows remain unavailable and cannot use this direct-reservation door.

Choose Stripe test or live mode, the reservation deposit in USD, an optional installment and its lead days, the balance lead days and an active follow-up owner. Live mode requires explicit confirmation. The remainder is calculated from the saved reservation total; the schedule cannot exceed that total. Lead days are calendar days before arrival in the home's time zone, at its arrival time. No provider call or charge is made while preparing the schedule.

The guest opens Payments from their existing verified stay portal. They see the exact amounts, dates, cancellation policy and mode, sign with their reservation name, explicitly authorize the schedule, and open Stripe's secure saved-card setup. Card details stay on Stripe. Returning guests use Check Stripe setup and payments to read the provider's confirmed setup. The installation needs the matching Stripe credentials; missing configuration fails before egress without exposing values. Managed hosting retains its existing unavailable-provider boundary.

The scheduler persists each due time and an uncertain attempt before contacting Stripe, using one stable idempotency key per attempt. Success requires the exact plan, job, attempt, currency and amount. It records one receipt and payment balance, and posts processor funds against receivables when the property's ledger is configured. Processor funds are not a bank receipt. Interrupted or ambiguous attempts only read provider records on restart, including all customer payment pages; an empty read never authorizes a second charge.

Failed or uncertain payments have a responsible operator, a linked task and a 24-hour follow-up deadline. Existing operator notices link to Payments; overdue review escalates once. A provider-confirmed failure with zero funds received can be reviewed and approved for one new attempt; the old attempt remains in history. An uncertain or authentication-required payment cannot use this retry door. Reconcile it in Stripe and retain the case for human review. For a new card, open Revise unpaid schedule or replace saved card. After every started effect is reconciled, review the remaining deposit/installment/balance, save it and have the guest approve and complete a fresh Stripe setup. Previous successful payments and all old attempts remain in history.

Under ct’s 22 October ruling, date, price, policy or property changes require fresh verified guest consent before another collection. A host amendment that only reduces the amount or defers the stay and its due dates in the guest’s favour retains the existing mandate: no payment increases or moves sooner. Reductions apply to unpaid jobs from the last payment backwards; successful payments and all attempts remain unchanged. A contact edit invalidates payment consent only when the normalized reservation email changes; a phone or name edit does not. Changed totals that require re-consent use Revise unpaid schedule to reconcile the remaining balance before a replacement mandate. The editor never resets successful payments. Failed and overdue follow-up continues as an assigned task and operator message while consent is invalid; it never authorizes a charge. A revised policy can be saved through the reservation amendment command, with audit history. Cancellation suppresses unstarted jobs, while already uncertain provider effects remain available for reconciliation. Revoked staff authority blocks queued charges. Manual or other receipts that exhaust the outstanding amount stop overcollection.

Archive exports remove saved-card capabilities and restore collection plans inactive. Guest erasure removes the mandate, consent and contact details and suppresses unstarted jobs. Historical payment references remain accounting history. No restored plan automatically resumes collection.

The provider-edge journey and `tests/collection-consent-flow.mjs` cover favourable reductions/deferrals, unchanged versus changed verification email, adverse dates/price/policy/home, verified renewal, continued follow-up without a charge, refusal of channel mandates through the direct door, and interruption both before provider acceptance and after an accepted charge whose response was lost, once-only overdue escalation, a revised remaining-balance mandate and revoked creator authority.

The unconditional integration assertion is in `tests/direct.test.mjs`. `node tests/collection-support.mjs` runs the provider-edge acceptance journey; it uses the repository's existing synthetic-cabin fixture convention at the intercepted Stripe network edge. Strict production credential validation is unchanged; no held key or live provider is used. The same journey runs unconditionally in Verify's direct-payment suite. `node tests/collection-browser.mjs` runs the same journey with desktop and phone browser proof, saving a deposit and pre-arrival balance through the Payments form at both widths. These network-edge journeys prove application behavior. The explicit held-key `tests/collection-stripe.mjs` journey additionally passed against real Stripe sandbox setup and payment records on 22 October 2026: deposit/installment/balance, response loss after acceptance and restart readback, a zero-received decline, assigned overdue task/message during invalid consent, replacement-card consent, changed arrival and cancellation. It observed five distinct provider attempts (four success, one decline), exact native receipts/balances and no repeated POST. Card entry occurs only on Stripe’s hosted setup page using Stripe’s documented sandbox cards. This does not establish live-mode funds, external guest mail delivery, Booking.com authorization or a physical lock. PAY-005/PAY-006 source-card paths remain unavailable.

Manual cash and cleared-check receipts are recorded in Payments → manual receipts. Enter the actual USD amount, receipt/clearance date, stable source reference and custody evidence; explicitly confirm that money was actually received. A pending/unverified check is not recorded as paid. The immutable trust-ledger entry reduces only that reservation's outstanding balance, refuses overpayment and conflicting replay, and never claims a Stripe settlement. Configure the property's ledger legal entity first. Correct erroneous evidence through the existing linked ledger reversal/correction door. Manual receipts do not complete PAY-001's pending/returned ACH acceptance.

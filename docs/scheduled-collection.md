# Scheduled reservation payments

Open Direct bookings → Payments · scheduled collection for a priced direct reservation created by staff. Its cancellation policy and guest email must already be saved. Existing direct website checkouts have their own payment flow and cannot be charged again here. Channel-paid reservations require their channel's dedicated payment mandate.

Choose Stripe test or live mode, the reservation deposit in USD cents, an optional installment and its lead days, the balance lead days and an active follow-up owner. Live mode requires explicit confirmation. The remainder is calculated from the saved reservation total; the schedule cannot exceed that total. Lead days are calendar days before arrival in the home's time zone, at its arrival time. No provider call or charge is made while preparing the schedule.

The guest opens Payments from their existing verified stay portal. They see the exact amounts, dates, cancellation policy and mode, sign with their reservation name, explicitly authorize the schedule, and open Stripe's secure saved-card setup. Card details stay on Stripe. Returning guests use Check Stripe setup and payments to read the provider's confirmed setup. The installation needs the matching Stripe credentials; missing configuration fails before egress without exposing values. Managed hosting retains its existing unavailable-provider boundary.

The scheduler persists each due time and an uncertain attempt before contacting Stripe, using one stable idempotency key per attempt. Success requires the exact plan, job, attempt, currency and amount. It records one receipt and payment balance, and posts processor funds against receivables when the property's ledger is configured. Processor funds are not a bank receipt. Interrupted or ambiguous attempts only read provider records on restart, including all customer payment pages; an empty read never authorizes a second charge.

Failed or uncertain payments have a responsible operator and a 24-hour follow-up deadline. Existing operator notices link to Payments; overdue review escalates once. A provider-confirmed failure with zero funds received can be reviewed and approved for one new attempt; the old attempt remains in history. An uncertain or authentication-required payment cannot use this retry door. Reconcile it in Stripe and retain the case for human review. This release does not yet replace an expired saved card through a new guest setup.

Any date, contact, total, policy or property change invalidates the saved mandate. Future due times move with the reservation, but collection pauses for fresh verified guest consent. Changed totals need human reconciliation before a replacement mandate; a schedule editor is still pending. This conservative re-consent policy is awaiting contract review. Cancellation suppresses unstarted jobs, while already uncertain provider effects remain available for reconciliation. Revoked staff authority blocks queued charges. Manual or other receipts that exhaust the outstanding amount stop overcollection.

Archive exports remove saved-card capabilities and restore collection plans inactive. Guest erasure removes the mandate, consent and contact details and suppresses unstarted jobs. Historical payment references remain accounting history. No restored plan automatically resumes collection.

The unconditional integration assertion is in `tests/direct.test.mjs`. `node tests/collection-support.mjs` runs the provider-edge acceptance journey; it requires a privately injected `CABIN_COLLECTION_TEST_CREDENTIAL` from the installation's Stripe test credential. It intercepts Stripe only at the network edge and stores no credential fixture. `node tests/collection-browser.mjs` runs the same journey with desktop and phone browser proof. These journeys prove application behavior, not live Stripe acceptance, real funds, delivery to a guest, Booking.com authorization or a physical lock.

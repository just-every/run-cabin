A turnover is one cleaning job between a guest checking out and the next arriving. A manager assigns a cleaner and a checklist to the departing booking; the cleaner completes every item and adds a photo, and the job shows as done for everyone with access.

## Prepare and assign a turnover

1. Open **Turnovers**. Under **Prepare a turnover**, choose the **Departing booking** (it lists each approved booking as home, title and checkout date) and the **Cleaner**.
2. Write the **Checklist**, one item per line, such as "Strip and remake beds" or "Restock coffee and towels". A checklist has from 1 to 50 different items.
3. Choose **Prepare turnover**. The job appears under **Turnover jobs** as a draft.
4. Check it and choose **Approve and assign turnover**. The cleaner now sees the job.

Only cleaners with active access appear in the list; add them in [Homes and people](/docs/homes-and-people). A booking has at most one approved turnover.

## What the cleaner does

The cleaner signs in on their phone or computer and opens **Turnovers**, where they see only their own jobs. For each job they:

1. Tick every checklist item.
2. Add a **Checklist photo**: one JPEG or PNG up to 256 KB.
3. Write a **Photo caption**, such as "Beds made, hot tub covered".
4. Choose **Complete photographed checklist**.

A job cannot be completed with an item unticked, and it cannot be completed twice.

## After the clean

The job shows **completed** with the time, the caption and a **View checklist photo** link. The photo is private: only the assigned cleaner and you and your managers can open it. If the booking changes after the job was assigned, the cleaner cannot complete the old assignment until a manager reviews the job again.

# Free source and provider costs

Cabin's source is published under the MIT licence. The authors sell no licence key, property or staff allowance, feature unlock or paid tier. Clone the public repository and use `rer dev` for an automatic local owner entry. A separate Cabin-author account is not part of installation or day-to-day operation.

Properties & people lets an owner maintain the whole portfolio and invite the team. A public-source installation was exercised with 40 homes and 25 active invited staff; all 20 linked product areas remained accessible. The browser used only the local installation. This is an access and licensing claim, not a statement that the separately tracked target capabilities or large-history performance requirements are complete.

In Channels, open **Your data & connected providers** before allowing an integration's actions and homes. Every provider declares its destination, purpose, shared data, provider-owned costs and a link to the provider's current terms. Review those costs before granting authority or saving a credential. Cabin collects none of these fees. Saving authority does not create a provider account or establish a live connection.

Hosting, channel commissions, payment/identity processing, mail, messaging, lock bridges, pricing and filing services can charge through the owner's own agreements. Guest rent paid through the host's processor is the host's booking revenue. Neither is a paid Cabin tier. Existing provider approvals and actual external outcomes remain the prerequisites named in CONTRACT.md.

Development suppresses invitations and other mail; local owner sign-in uses the automatic entry printed by rer. Hosted invitations use the owner's configured mail provider.

Open **Direct bookings** in the installed Cabin workspace. Configure each home's public address, nightly rate, cleaning and extra-guest fees, occupancy, minimum/maximum nights and minimum gap. A gap of zero is allowed next to an existing reservation; other gaps must meet the configured minimum. Publish the home to make `/book/<address>` available. `/book` lists the published homes. Calendar availability includes channel stays, blocks and pending direct checkouts. No guest names appear on public availability pages.

Set `STRIPE_TEST_SECRET_KEY` privately on the installation. Deploy declares it as an optional secret; without it, Cabin explains that Stripe must be configured. This edition accepts Stripe **test mode only**, not real charges. Self-hosted installations call Stripe directly. On RER Managed, payments, deposits and Identity say “not available yet” until a provider relay exists; no provider request is attempted.

A guest selects dates and guests, reviews nightly charges, fees, each configured state/county tax and the separate damage authorization. The guest signs the exact version of the home's agreement and consents to payment, card storage for the damage hold, and Identity when required. Cabin retains the document, signature, time, version, consent and SHA-256 with the stay. Guest and authorized staff can download that copy. Changing home settings never changes a saved quote or signature.

Checkout reserves nights atomically in Cabin's shared calendar, then redirects to Stripe's hosted card form. Card numbers never pass through Cabin. Success is established by reading Stripe's paid Checkout Session and successful USD PaymentIntent, not by trusting a browser return parameter. Repeating a refresh cannot create another rent charge or damage hold. A five-minute job reconciles abandoned/paid checkouts; unpaid expired sessions reopen nights. The guest can refresh the booking link immediately. Cabin's exports for Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com reflect the held nights immediately. External calendars consume those exports on their own polling schedule; partner API guarantees are not available.

After rent payment, Cabin places a separate manual-capture authorization on the saved card. Required Stripe Identity checks run through Stripe's actual test-mode flow; Cabin stores only the provider reference and state, never ID images. A stay remains pending until rent, agreement, required verified identity and required authorization all succeed. Incomplete/failed checks and provider errors remain visible to a manager in Direct bookings. The route tests use synthetic Stripe responses only at the network edge; the installed runtime always contacts Stripe.

For an arrived stay, staff record actual checkout with **Mark guest checked out**. Otherwise the scheduled checkout date ends at 23:59 UTC. After checkout, the scheduled job releases an unclaimed authorization within the home's configured release hours (plus up to five minutes for the job). Staff can release after checkout or capture a documented damage amount with a reason and evidence reference. Capture releases the unclaimed remainder. The provider's actual expiry appears on the stay; an authorization expiring before checkout is flagged for the host to arrange a replacement. Cabin does not silently renew it. A lost settlement response is reconciled by refresh rather than allowing a second financial action.

Tax is calculated using the home's configured basis-point rates on rent and all fees; it is itemized and collected in Checkout. Configure the right rates and applicability for the property. State and county are separate lines and separate manual filing records. After filing and remitting through the relevant authority, staff record its confirmation reference against the stay. Cabin does **not** file or remit through Avalara or another tax provider. Exemptions, city levies, refunds, tax adjustment returns and automated filings remain open contract work.

The broader contract also still requires live payments/ACH, installment schedules, refunds/cancellations, agreement amendments and renewed signatures, a branded custom-domain site with galleries/search, an authorized embed, and manual identity review controls. The Cabin marketing homepage is unchanged.
